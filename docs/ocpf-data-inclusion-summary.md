## MAPLE Finance Tab — Data Inclusion Summary

This summarizes what campaign finance data MAPLE uses from OCPF's public bulk data files, what we include vs. exclude, and why.

Source data: OCPF's `reports.txt` (one row per filed report) and `report-items.txt` (one row per individual transaction). Our research and decisions were based on files for the current election cycle only (2025–2026).

### Report types: what we include/exclude from `reports.txt` (totals use an allowlist strategy, exclusive by default)

| Report Type                                                                    | Included in Total Raised/Spent?                                 | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **60 – Deposit Report**                                                        | No (totals excluded) — but its items ARE used for the breakdown | Carries gross amounts, before processing fees. The same money reappears net-of-fees on the Bank Report; counting both would double it. We still pull its individual transactions (see below) and its unitemized total.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **70 – Bank Report**                                                           | **Yes — primary source**                                        | This is the actual bank reconciliation: net of fees, matches real cash. Cash on Hand comes from here, as does most of Total Raised and Total Spent (the rest is External Activity Report totals and out-of-pocket items, see below). Total Raised then has non-contribution receipts (type 204, see below) netted back out, to match OCPF's own public "Receipts" definition.                                                                                                     |
| **13 – External Activity Report**                                              | **Yes** — and its items are used                                | Activity outside the depository account, so it never shows up in a Bank Report. Its items also include candidate out-of-pocket expenses (331/332), but those are _not_ in the report's own totals; they're added to Total Raised/Spent separately (see below). In the 2026 data, 15 House/Senate filers filed one; only 2 had non-zero receipts ($10,130.66 combined), and expenditures totaled $464.85. Not yet checked against a Year-End Report (2025 type-13 totals were $0). |
| **61 – Late Contribution Report**                                              | No (totals excluded) — but its items ARE used                   | Pre-election disclosure of contributions that are then deposited and bank-reported. Totals were $0 in the 2025–2026 data anyway.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **63 – Subvendor Report**                                                      | No (totals excluded) — but its items ARE used                   | Supplemental: itemizes spending the Bank Report already counts. **Confirmed double count**: for all 9 legislators who filed one in 2025, summed totals matched the Year-End Report only once type 63 was excluded (e.g. CPF 17791 was overstated by $17,734). 2026: $255,692 across 7 legislators.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **65 – Payroll Itemization Report**                                            | No (totals excluded) — but its items ARE used                   | Supplemental: itemizes payroll spending the Bank Report already counts. One legislator, $1,212 in 2026.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **80 – Credit Card Report**                                                    | No                                                              | Supplemental — itemizes spending the Bank Report already counts. Verified empirically: summing Bank Reports alone matches Year-End totals exactly without these.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **90 – Reimbursement Report**                                                  | No                                                              | Same reasoning as Credit Card reports.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **11 / 24 / 32 / 36 / 45 / 52 / 113 – Year-End Reports** (all committee types) | No — but its in-kind items ARE used                             | Annual rollups that duplicate the periodic (Bank Report) totals already summed. Used as a sanity check — we compare our summed totals against the Year-End figure and log a warning if they differ by more than 2 cents. The comparison uses raw report totals, before the 204 and 331/332 item adjustments, since Year-End totals include 204 cash and exclude out-of-pocket items. Also the main source of in-kind contributions (see below).                                   |
| **12 – Dissolution Report, 14/15 – Transition-Out/In Report**                  | No — but their in-kind items ARE used                           | **Same double-counting risk as Year-End, confirmed with a real example**: one committee's Dissolution Report exactly matched the sum of its prior two Bank Reports ($1,583.64 to the penny). Not tied to a calendar year, so tracked separately from the Year-End reconciliation.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **62 – Segregated Account Report**                                             | N/A                                                             | Never filed by any state legislator — used by a different class of committee (18 filers statewide, all non-legislators). Never reaches our logic since we only process CPF IDs already mapped to a sitting legislator.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Anything else not listed above                                                 | **No — excluded by default** (items ARE used)                   | Totals come only from an allowlist (70, 13). An unfamiliar report type is left out of the totals, and the scraper logs a warning ("Unclassified OCPF report type") so someone can classify it. Its items and its `Receipts_Unitemized_Total` are still used, the same as for 60/61/63/65.                                                                                                                                                                                         |

### Where the data from `reports.txt` is used in the UI:

| UI field                                                             | Source in `reports.txt`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Total Raised**                                                     | Sum of `Receipts_Total` from type-70 Bank Reports and type-13 External Activity Reports only, plus out-of-pocket items (331/332), minus items coded 204 (Non-contribution receipts, such as refunds, etc.) dated on or before the latest Bank Report `End_Date`. Items come from `report-items.txt`     |
| **Total Spent**                                                      | Sum of `Expenditures_Total` from type-70 and type-13 reports only (same allowlist as Total Raised), plus out-of-pocket items (331/332)                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Cash on Hand**                                                     | `End_Balance` of the type-70 Bank Report with the latest `End_Date`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Contributions Breakdown → "Micro-donations (under $50)"**          | `Receipts_Unitemized_Total`, from every report type _except_ the Year-End/Dissolution/Transition rollups and the Credit Card/Reimbursement (80/90) supplements. Unlike Total Raised/Spent, type 60 is included here, since this field is blank in the bank reports                                      |
| **"Through" date in the Total Raised footnote**                      | taken from the type-70 Bank Report's `End_Date`. (`bankDataAsOf` in code). Omitted from Firestore when the legislator has no Bank Report. Total Spent's "As of" date is not from a report: it's the month the scraper last ran (`lastUpdated`). Cash on Hand shows no date ("End of reporting period"). |
| **"Includes contributions through" date on Contributions Breakdown** | taken from the type-60 Deposit Report's `End_Date`. (`depositDataAsOf` in code). Omitted from Firestore when the legislator has no Deposit Report.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **"Small Donors" %**                                                 | Partly — combines unitemized amount above with item code 201 (<$200)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

- `startBalance` is also captured from `reports.txt` (type-70 `Start_Balance`, earliest `Start_Date`) and written to Firestore, but per caveat #1 below, it is **not currently displayed** on the tab. If a committee has two Bank Reports with the same earliest `Start_Date` (e.g. two accounts), whichever is read first is used. For CPF 14454 (Brownsberger) that gives $0.00, from one of his two January 2025 Bank Reports.

- `years[y].finalized`, whether a type-11/24/32/36/45/52/113 Year-End report has been seen for that year, is also captured and written to Firestore, but it is not currently displayed on the tab, and nothing in the frontend reads it yet.

- Everything else on the tab (Individual/Committee/Union donors, Candidate's own funds, In-kind, contributions count) comes from `report-items.txt` instead.

### A note about inclusion/exclusion models:

Report totals (Total Raised/Spent) and item codes both use allowlists: nothing is counted unless we've identified what it is.

For `reports.txt` (report type codes), only Bank Reports (70) and External Activity Reports (13) feed Total Raised/Spent. Between them they cover all of a committee's activity: money through the depository account, and money outside it. Every other report type either rolls up (Year-End, Dissolution/Transition) or itemizes (Deposit, Credit Card, Reimbursement, Subvendor, Payroll) activity that is already in those two. Every report type's handling (totals, items only, Year-End, lifecycle, or skip), along with the reason, is defined in one map in the scraper: `REPORT_TYPE_HANDLING`.

For `report-items.txt` (transaction/record codes), a transaction only counts toward a category if its code is explicitly included (201, 202, 203, 204, 206, 331, 332, 401–403, 420, 319) (Detailed in above table). Any other code, including any OCPF introduces later, is silently skipped.

**Which reports' items get read is a separate question from which reports' totals count.** `report-items.txt` has no report-type column, so the scraper reads an item only if its parent report was registered while parsing `reports.txt`. Every report type is registered except Credit Card/Reimbursement (80/90). Year-End and Dissolution/Transition reports are registered for in-kind items (401/402/403/420) only. They are the main place in-kind contributions are itemized, and Transition-In reports also carry contribution and 204 items that would otherwise leak into the breakdown and Total Raised. So, for example, Deposit Report (60) totals are excluded but its items supply almost all of the Contributions Breakdown.

Reasoning for this choice:

We originally used a blocklist for report totals, on the theory that excluding an unfamiliar report type risked undercounting. In practice the opposite happened: Subvendor Reports (63), which weren't on the blocklist, were silently double-counting expenditures for several legislators. A double count looks plausible and goes unnoticed. An undercount is caught by the Year-End reconciliation, and the scraper also logs a warning for any unclassified report type. Checked against the 2025 Year-End Reports, the allowlist matches for 409 of 413 legislators versus 400 under the old blocklist. The remaining 4 have missing or partial Bank Reports.

`report-items.txt` is used for the Contributions Breakdown list, adding only to specific categories (Individual donors, Committees/PACs, etc). Since there's no generic "misc" bucket a transaction can safely fall into, a code is only counted once we know which category it belongs to.

### What we show on a legislator's Finance tab

- **Total Raised** — net receipts, "as of" the most recent Bank Report on file, plus candidate out-of-pocket expenses, minus non-contribution receipts (refunds/misc). Matches OCPF's own public "Receipts" figure.
- **Total Spent** — net expenditures for the cycle, including candidate out-of-pocket expenses
- The logic was verified to the penny against ocpf.us's "2026 YTD" view: the 2026 per-year totals (`years["2026"].totalRaised`/`totalSpent`), the `Start_Balance` of the first 2026 Bank Report, and Cash on Hand. Note that the tab's headline Total Raised/Spent are the sum of both years (2025 + 2026), and `startBalance` is the start of 2025, so those won't match ocpf.us's 2026 YTD figures directly.
- **Cash on Hand** — most recent Bank Report's ending balance
- **Small Donors** — donations under $200 as a % of the Contributions Breakdown total (which also includes Candidate's own funds and In-kind contributions)
- **Contributions Breakdown** — a table of: Individual donors, Committees/PACs, Unions & associations, Micro-donations (under $50), Candidate's own funds, In-kind contributions — each shown with dollar amount and % share, "as of" the most recent Deposit Report on file (Deposit Report items are the main source; in-kind contributions come mainly from Year-End Reports, and some items from External Activity Reports)
- Footnote disclosing processing fees (why the breakdown total is larger than Total Raised), shown only when a legislator has processing fees

### Known caveats

Of these, only the processing fees (#2), the "as of" dates (#2), the Small Donors definition (#4, as "Under $200 contributions") and the contributor-count footnote (#5) are explained on the tab itself.

1. **Scope is 2025–2026 only.** We don't display money carried over from a prior cycle ("Start Balance"). It is captured as `startBalance`, but not shown on the tab. This means **Total Spent can legitimately exceed Total Raised** for a legislator who started the cycle with leftover cash. (Confirmed with a real example: one legislator's numbers only balance once you back into a ~$45K starting balance from the prior cycle.)
2. **Total Raised (net) will typically differ from the sum of the Contributions Breakdown (gross).** The main differences are:

   - Processing fees: the breakdown is gross, Total Raised is net of fees.
   - Money itemized but not yet bank-reported: a normal, ongoing lag, because Deposit Reports are filed per transaction and Bank Reports on a fixed periodic schedule.
   - In-kind contributions: they're in the breakdown but never in Total Raised, since no money changes hands.

   For a filer with only Bank and Deposit Reports and no in-kind contributions, this was validated as an exact identity:
   `Total Raised = Breakdown Sum − Processing Fees − Money Itemized But Not Yet Bank-Reported`
   Both figures carry their own "as of" date on the tab for transparency.

3. **Cash on Hand will not exactly equal Start Balance + Total Raised − Total Spent**, off by the amount of non-contribution receipts for the period. Cash on Hand and Total Spent both come straight from the Bank Report (which includes non-contribution cash that actually hit the account), while Total Raised has that same amount netted out (see above) to match OCPF's own "Receipts" figure. This is expected — OCPF's own displayed numbers have the identical property (confirmed against ocpf.us for CPF 16883). External Activity Report totals also break the equation, since that money never passes through the bank. Out-of-pocket expenses (331/332) don't, because they're added to both Total Raised and Total Spent and cancel out.
4. **"Small Donors" (<$200) is defined as**: itemized individual contributions under $200 (record type 201, amount < $200) plus the report-level unitemized aggregate (contributions ≤ $50, which by law don't need to be itemized at all). This lines up with MA's real $200/year cumulative threshold for when a committee must start disclosing a donor's occupation/employer.
5. **"From N contributors" under Total Raised is a unique-contributor count** (`uniqueContributorsCount`), covering itemized individual, committee and union contributions (201/202/203) across the whole election cycle. OCPF has no contributor ID, so a contributor is matched on name + first name + 5-digit ZIP, with the record type kept separate so a person never merges with an organization. Street address isn't used because its formatting varies between filings for the same person ("St" vs "Street"). Nicknames ("Dan"/"Daniel") and contributors who moved are counted more than once. Small donations a campaign reports only as a lump sum (`Receipts_Unitemized_Total`) have no donor names, so they can't be counted; the tab says so in a footnote. Itemizing them is optional and most campaigns do it anyway: in the 2025 data, 362,148 of 460,462 individual contribution rows (about 79%) were $50 or less, so most small donors are counted. The stored `contributionsCount` is the transaction count for the same record types (a person giving 5 times counts as 5); it isn't displayed.
